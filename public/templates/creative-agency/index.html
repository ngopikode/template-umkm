Harvesting SOP

Standard Operating Protocols

We cooperate with regional farm unions to lock in crops at their harvest peaks. Standardizing slow drying, moisture checks, and hand-sorting steps yields premium agricultural exports.

Ethical Direct Sourcing

Buying directly from farm collectives ensures fair crop pricing for growers and enables complete soil pesticide trail monitoring.

Slow Shade Drying

Cacao and vanilla undergo slow shade curing to allow complex sugars to stabilize, maintaining visual pod oils.

Nitrogen-Flushed Packs

Vacuum food-grade bags or nitrogen-flushed packages preserve aromatic oils and moisture tolerances during ocean freight.
